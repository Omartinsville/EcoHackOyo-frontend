TRACK 01 · HACKATHON

Register as a Hackathon Contestant.

Have an idea that can solve a real economic problem? Build with a team, get mentored, and pitch for a shot at showcasing live at the EcoHackOyo Main Event in Ibadan.

📅 September 2026 · 4 Weeks 💻 Online · Zoom + WhatsApp 🆓 Free — ₦0 🎟️ 500 Slots Only

What you'll do

Across four weeks you will identify a real economic challenge, research it, build an innovative solution with a team, and pitch it before a panel of judges.

  • 01 Identify a real economic challenge
  • 02 Research & understand the problem
  • 03 Develop an innovative solution
  • 04 Build with a team, with mentor support
  • 05 Create & present your solution
  • 06 Pitch before a panel of judges

Top 20 participants showcase live at the Main Event on October 22–23 in Ibadan, before government representatives, investors and sponsors.

ℹ️ Registration is free. Only 500 slots are available for the September 2026 cohort — early applications are prioritized.
